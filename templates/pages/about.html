About Servana

Trusted local services, made easy across New Zealand.

Who we are

Servana is a New Zealand service marketplace. We bring customers, businesses and local service providers together in one place, so finding, comparing, booking and paying for a job is simple and safe. Whether you need a cleaner in Auckland, a plumber in Wellington, a tutor in Dunedin or a photographer in Queenstown, Servana helps you find someone you can trust.

Our mission

To make everyday services easier to find and safer to buy across New Zealand, and to give local providers a fair way to reach new customers. We believe nobody should have to chase quotes, worry about scams or pay someone they cannot trust.

What you can find

Our categories cover home maintenance, cleaning, vehicle services, moving and delivery, technical help, building and construction, outdoor and rural work, pets, education, health and wellbeing, family and care, events and creative work, business and finance, energy and healthy homes, and personal services.

How it works

1. Join and verify

Sign up, then confirm your email, mobile number and passport/visa. An admin checks your documents.

2. Find and compare

Search by service and city, compare prices and ratings, or ask for a quote.

3. Book and pay safely

Pay in full or an advance. Servana holds the money, not the provider.

4. Confirm and review

When the work is done you confirm it, we release the payment, and you can leave a review.

Who Servana is for

Customers

Individuals who need a job done. Compare providers, book online, pay securely and review the work.

Service providers

People and companies that offer services. Get found by local customers, receive requests and quotes in one dashboard and build a reputation with reviews.

Businesses

Companies that need services. Request work, track bookings and monthly spending, and keep a clear record of every job.

Admins

The Servana team. We verify people, hold and release payments, answer complaints and keep the marketplace fair.

How Servana keeps you safe

  • Verified people only. Every account confirms its email, mobile number and passport/visa, and an admin approves the documents. Each passport can be used on one account only.
  • Protected payments. You pay Servana first. The provider starts after your payment is received, and is paid only after you confirm the work is done.
  • No off-site deals. Links, emails, phone numbers and requests to pay outside Servana are blocked in messages, quotes, reviews and descriptions.
  • Fair reviews. Only customers with a completed booking can review a provider, and the admin can hide unfair reviews.
  • Complaints handled. Our admin team reviews every complaint and can refund a payment.
  • Private documents. Passport and visa photos are stored privately and only you and the admin can open them.
